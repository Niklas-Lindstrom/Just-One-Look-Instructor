Frequently Asked Questions

What if I don't find anything when I look?

That's completely normal. Not finding anything is still a valid observation. The point is to try and notice what actually happens, whether that's something or nothing.

How long should I look?

Anywhere from 30 seconds to a few minutes. There's no requirement. Do whatever feels natural to you.

Do I need to be in a special state of mind?

No. You can do this while you're tired, stressed, angry, happy, or neutral. Your actual state is what you're observing.

Can I do this while busy?

You need a quiet moment to focus. But it doesn't have to be long. Even a few minutes is fine.

What if I fall asleep?

That's information too. You discovered that you were tired. Just try again when you're more alert.

Is this meditation?

No. Meditation usually involves specific techniques or maintaining a particular state. This is just observation. You're not trying to achieve anything.

Will this change me?

Maybe, maybe not. We don't make promises. You're doing an observation exercise and seeing what you notice. What happens after that is up to you.

How often should I do this?

As often as you want. Once a day, once a week, multiple times a day—it's entirely your choice. There's no requirement.

Can I do this with other practices?

Yes. This is a simple observation exercise. You can do it alongside other things if you want.

What if I have questions during the practice?

You can use the interactive chatbot guide on this website. It's designed to help you explore your experience in a practical way.

Do I need to believe anything?

No. This isn't about belief. It's about your own direct observation. What you find is what you find.

Is there a right way to do this?

Your way is the right way. You're looking at yourself and noticing what happens. That's it.

What if nothing seems to happen?

Then nothing seemed to happen. That's your observation. Notice that, and see if there's anything else worth noting about the experience.

Can the chatbot replace a therapist or counselor?

No. The chatbot is here to help you with the inward look exercise. If you're dealing with mental health issues, please speak with a qualified professional.

Is this expensive?

No. The website and guide are free. You don't pay anything.
