What Is the Just One Look Method?

The Basics

The Just One Look Method is a simple, practical approach to understanding yourself through direct observation.

It's not a spiritual practice, meditation system, religion, or philosophy. It's a straightforward psychological exercise.

Two Simple Steps

Step 1: Looking at Yourself

Direct your attention toward the immediate feeling of being you. Not your ideas about yourself, but the actual feeling of existing as you, right now.

Step 2: The Self-Directed Attention Exercise

Practice intentionally directing your attention. This strengthens your ability to focus and observe your own experience.

What It's Not

  • Not a path to happiness or transformation
  • Not a belief system or religion
  • Not meditation or contemplative practice
  • Not a philosophy or ideology
  • Not a way to escape your life or avoid problems

What It Is

  • A simple observation exercise
  • A way to notice how you actually feel
  • A straightforward psychological approach
  • Self-reliant and independent
  • Grounded in direct observation

Why Do It?

People do the inward look for different reasons:

  • To understand themselves better
  • To notice how they actually feel moment to moment
  • To develop their ability to observe and focus
  • Out of curiosity about their own experience
  • Because it's simple and costs nothing

There's no requirement or expectation. You do it if you want to. That's it.

How Often?

There's no schedule. You can do the inward look:

  • Once a day
  • Multiple times a day
  • Once a week
  • Whenever you feel like it

It's entirely up to you. This is about your own observation and decision-making.

Getting Help

This website provides:

  • A step-by-step guide to do the inward look
  • An interactive chatbot to help you explore your experience
  • Answers to common questions

The goal is to help you actually do the practice, not to explain it endlessly.
