The Inward Look

This is a simple, practical exercise. Follow these steps.

Step 1: Settle In

Find a quiet place where you can sit comfortably for a few minutes. You don't need anything special. Just a moment to yourself.

Once you're ready, go to Step 2.

Step 2: Look at Yourself

Direct your attention inward. Notice the immediate feeling of being you. Not your thoughts about yourself, but the direct feeling of existing as you, right now.

Look at this for a moment. Just notice.

Take 30 seconds to 2 minutes for this step. There's no right or wrong here.

Step 3: Notice What You Find

What did you notice? What was that like? Did you find anything? Did you find nothing? Did something else happen?

Just notice what occurred.

Reflect on Your Experience

Here are some questions you can think about:

  • What did you notice when you looked?
  • What was the feeling of being you like?
  • Did anything surprise you?
  • Was it easy to do, or difficult?
  • What would you do differently next time?

You don't need to answer these perfectly. Just reflect on what happened.
